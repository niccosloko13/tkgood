# Babi

## Product intelligence for TikTok

Babi finds products beginning to gain strength in TikTok search results and
shows the observable evidence behind that attention.

## What it is

- A search-only product-intelligence workspace.
- A way to compare repeated product signals across searches and creators.
- A transparent view of opportunities and their evidence limits.

## What it is not

- A social engagement bot.
- A social-media manager.
- A supplier marketplace or ecommerce platform.
- An indiscriminate scraper.
- A replacement for human commercial judgement.

## Safety

The browser bridge is read-only. Direct video enrichment remains separately
controlled, and no product screen may weaken the Safety Contract.
